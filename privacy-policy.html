1. What information do we collect?

Information you provide directly:

  • Email address and password when you create an account
  • Links (URLs) you save to your shelf
  • Notes you add to saved links
  • Collection names and visibility settings you create
  • Reminders and alert preferences you set

Information collected automatically:

  • Log data including IP address, device type, operating system, and app version
  • Usage data such as which features you use and when
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information

Information from third-party sources:

When you save a link, our servers may fetch publicly available metadata from that webpage — such as the page title, description, and Open Graph tags — in order to display a useful summary. We do not store the full content of web pages, only the metadata needed to identify and describe the link.

2. How do we use your information?

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the App
  • Create and manage your account
  • Save, organise, and display your links
  • Generate AI-powered summaries and categories for your saved links
  • Generate personalised recommendations in the Discover tab based on your saved content
  • Send you reminders and notifications you have set up
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We do not use your information for targeted advertising and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. AI processing and recommendations

ShelfIt uses Anthropic's Claude AI to automatically generate summaries and categories for links you save. When you save a link, the page title, description, and URL are sent to Anthropic's API for processing. Anthropic processes this data in accordance with their own privacy policy and terms of service.

The Discover tab uses your saved link history to generate personalised recommendations. This analysis is performed by Claude AI and is based solely on the content you have saved. We do not share your data with advertisers or use it to build advertising profiles.

AI-generated summaries, categories, and recommendations are provided for convenience and may not always be accurate. They do not constitute professional advice of any kind.

By using the App, you consent to your saved link data being processed by Anthropic for the purposes described above.

4. Affiliate links

ShelfIt may modify certain links you save to include affiliate tracking codes from programmes such as Amazon Associates, Booking.com, Awin, and others. This means we may earn a small commission if you make a purchase through a saved link, at no additional cost to you.

We will never overwrite an existing affiliate or referral code belonging to a creator, influencer, or third party. Affiliate tracking is applied only to links from participating retailers where no existing affiliate code is already present in the URL.

Affiliate link transformation happens automatically when a link is saved and processed. The transformed URL is stored in your shelf in place of the original.

5. When do we share your information?

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

Service providers: We share data with the following third-party providers who help us operate the App:

  • Supabase — database storage and authentication
  • Anthropic — AI summarisation and recommendations
  • Vercel — hosting for public collection pages
  • Expo / EAS — app build and distribution
  • Apple — push notifications and app delivery

Public collections: If you choose to make a collection public, its name and contents will be visible to other ShelfIt users and accessible via a public URL. You can change a collection back to private at any time.

Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ShelfIt, our users, or the public.

Business transfers: If ShelfIt is acquired or merged with another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. International data transfers

ShelfIt is operated from the United Kingdom. Our service providers, including Supabase and Anthropic, may process your data in the United States and other countries. These countries may not have data protection laws equivalent to those in the UK or EEA.

Where we transfer data internationally, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including relying on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission where applicable.

7. How long do we keep your information?

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data from our active systems within 30 days. Some information may be retained in backup systems for a limited period for fraud prevention and legal compliance purposes.

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the App.

8. How do we keep your information safe?

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission over HTTPS
  • Supabase Row Level Security policies ensuring users can only access their own data
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Session tokens stored securely in the device keychain

Despite these measures, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and you use the App at your own risk.

9. Do we collect information from minors?

ShelfIt is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us at shelf.links@gmail.com.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at shelf.links@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the UK or EEA and believe we are processing your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Legal basis for processing (UK/EU users): We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the App and its features
  • Consent — for AI processing of your saved links
  • Legitimate interests — for security, fraud prevention, and improving the App
  • Legal obligation — where required by law
